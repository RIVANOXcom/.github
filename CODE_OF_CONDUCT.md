
# RIVANOX Code of Conduct

## Purpose

RIVANOX encourages respectful, constructive and professional communication regarding software development, technical documentation and practical digital solutions.

Technical feedback, improvement suggestions, bug reports and relevant discussions are welcome.

## Expected Behavior

Participants should:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Provide constructive technical feedback.
- Respect different perspectives and experiences.
- Keep discussions relevant and actionable.
- Protect privacy and confidential information.
- Respect intellectual property and applicable laws.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following conduct is not permitted:

- Harassment, discrimination or threats
- Personal attacks or abusive communication
- Spam or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Unauthorized disclosure of private information
- Publication of credentials, tokens or license keys
- Public disclosure of confidential vulnerabilities
- Misuse of repository communication channels

## Security and Privacy

Sensitive information must not be published in public issues, discussions or pull requests.

Security concerns should follow the applicable SECURITY.md policy.

## Moderation

Repository maintainers may close, lock, hide or remove inappropriate contributions where permitted by GitHub.

Participation may be restricted when necessary to protect constructive technical discussion.

## Contact

Questions or concerns regarding community conduct can be submitted through the official RIVANOX support channels.

[https://rivanox.com/contact/](https://rivanox.com/contact/)

## Development Philosophy

Professional communication, technical clarity and mutual respect contribute to better software.

**Powerful Software. Practical by Design.**
